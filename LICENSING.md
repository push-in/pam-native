You need a commercial license from Pushin to make production use of PAM in a
product or service whose primary purpose is to substitute for PAM as an
application development framework, developer platform, runtime environment, or
UI engine. Offering a rebranded or repackaged standalone PAM is included.

The BSL 1.1 base terms still permit copying, modification, derivative works,
redistribution, and non-production use. The restriction applies to production
use outside the Additional Use Grant.
